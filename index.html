About Us

Founded and managed by Charlotte Singleton, Binx & Co is a forward-thinking London based communications hub providing PR, marketing and brand consultancy to chefs, restaurants, pubs and lifestyle brands across the UK.

With an in-house approach, Charlotte works closely with clients to develop bespoke and creative integrated communication strategies that deliver impactful press coverage, as well as driving sales to all core parts of your business. With over a decade worth of experience and having worked with some of the best in the business, Charlotte also integrates clients into her multi-faceted industry network.
